How we approach the US

Localize with discipline.

The goal is not to translate labels and pretend every service is ready. Each product earns its launch through verified functionality, contracts, privacy, accessibility and support readiness.

01 · PRESERVE

Protect Brazil

Separate development branches, region-specific configuration and no unauthorized production merges.

02 · ADAPT

Make it local

English interfaces first; US-specific infrastructure and legal requirements validated separately.

03 · VALIDATE

Earn readiness

Automated builds, regression checks and careful real-user pilots before commercial launch.
